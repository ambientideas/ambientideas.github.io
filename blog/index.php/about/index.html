I’m Matthew McCullough. These days I run product for Android’s developer tools at Google, as VP of Product Management, Android Developer. My teams cover product management, research, UX design, community, and developer relations, and the work spans Android Studio, Kotlin, and the APIs Android developers use every day.

Before that I was at GitHub for several years, first building out the Professional Services organization and later as SVP of Product.

Ambient Ideas was the consultancy I co-founded before either of those. Through the 2000s and into 2011 we did software architecture, open source consulting, and developer training, mostly out of Denver. The posts on this blog are from that stretch. Leaving them up as an archive.

Wrote a fair amount along the way. I’m a co-author on three O’Reilly books, Version Control with Git, Building and Testing with Gradle, and Jenkins: The Definitive Guide, and on Presentation Patterns for Addison-Wesley. Also made the Git Master Class video series. Gave several hundred conference talks over the years, at No Fluff Just Stuff, JavaOne, JFokus, JavaZone, and Devnexus.

Current work and contact details are at matthewjmccullough.com.
